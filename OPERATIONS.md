# OPERATIONS MANUAL

*Day-to-day operational procedures for the Automated Fleet.*
*This document is project law, not constitutional law.*

---

## PURPOSE

This Operations Manual governs the practical, day-to-day execution of work within the Fleet. It is subordinate to the Constitution and subsidiary laws. It may be amended by the Tech Lead and Reviewer without escalation, provided changes do not conflict with higher law.

---

## 1. WORKFLOW

### 1.1 Task Assignment

1. The Tech Lead decomposes project requirements into discrete tasks.
2. Tasks are assigned to Implementers based on capability and availability.
3. The Tech Lead determines whether tasks may be parallelized per Article V of the Constitution and Subsidiary Law 005.

### 1.2 Implementation Cycle

1. **Plan** — The Implementer reviews the task, consults relevant interfaces (Article IV), and plans their approach. Implementers have broad latitude in planning.
2. **Execute** — The Implementer builds the solution within the bounds of project law and frozen interfaces.
3. **Self-Review** — Before submitting for review, the Implementer verifies compliance with applicable laws and interfaces.
4. **Peer Review** — The Reviewer evaluates the work. The Review Response Protocol (Subsidiary Law 001) governs the interaction.
5. **Integrate** — Accepted work is merged into the main branch.

### 1.3 Dependency Management

1. When a task depends on the output of another, the dependent Implementer shall wait rather than proceed with assumptions.
2. If a dependency is blocked, the Tech Lead shall be notified to reassess priorities.
3. Idle time due to genuine dependencies is acceptable (Article V, Section 2).

---

## 2. COMMUNICATION

### 2.1 Channels

1. All substantive technical discussion shall be documented in writing.
2. Decisions made in discussion shall be recorded — either as task comments, interface amendments, or precedent entries as appropriate.

### 2.2 Status Reporting

1. Implementers shall report progress at intervals defined by the project cadence.
2. Blockers shall be reported immediately to the Tech Lead.
3. The Tech Lead shall maintain a current view of project status for all Advocates.

---

## 3. ATTORNEY GENERAL REVIEWS

### 3.1 Cooperation

1. All agents shall cooperate with Attorney General retrospective reviews (Subsidiary Law 004).
2. When the Attorney General requests information or explanation, agents shall respond promptly and substantively.
3. Agents have the right to defend their actions per Article III of the Constitution.

### 3.2 Remediation

1. When a deviation is identified, the responsible agent shall propose a remediation plan.
2. The Attorney General may accept the plan or recommend alternatives.
3. Persistent or willful deviation shall be escalated to the Group Deciders.

---

## 4. INTERFACE MANAGEMENT

### 4.1 Registry

1. All frozen interfaces shall be recorded in the project's interface registry.
2. The registry shall include: interface name, specification, freeze date, dependent parties, and amendment history.

### 4.2 Lifecycle

1. Interfaces begin as **draft** — open to change without formal process.
2. When parties agree on stability, an interface is **frozen** per Subsidiary Law 003.
3. Frozen interfaces may be **amended** through the process defined in Subsidiary Law 003.
4. Interfaces no longer in use may be **retired** by agreement of all dependent parties.

---

## 5. AMENDMENT OF THIS DOCUMENT

1. This Operations Manual may be amended by the Tech Lead and Reviewer through mutual agreement.
2. Amendments that conflict with the Constitution or subsidiary laws are void.
3. Amendments shall be documented with date and rationale.
